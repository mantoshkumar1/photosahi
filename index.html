Private photo preparation

Passport, OCI, PCC and Canadian application photo maker

Private browser-based photo preparation for passport, OCI, PCC, Canadian applications, LinkedIn and Microsoft Teams. PhotoSahi assists with sizing and framing; the receiving organization makes the final acceptance decision.

Available photo presets

  • Canadian Citizenship / Passport 600 × 840 px output preset
  • Indian Passport (Reissue) 600 × 600 px output preset
  • Indian Passport Surrender 600 × 600 px output preset
  • Indian OCI 600 × 600 px output preset
  • Indian PCC 600 × 600 px output preset
  • LinkedIn Profile 800 × 800 px output preset
  • Microsoft Teams 800 × 800 px output preset

How your photo stays private

Your local file is opened in the browser, converted from HEIC or HEIF if needed, checked for a face, cropped and adjusted, then encoded as a metadata-free JPEG for local download.

The optional feedback form sends only the category, message and optional email that you type. It does not attach your photo.

Important limitations

  • PhotoSahi assists with sizing and framing but cannot guarantee acceptance by any receiving authority.
  • Face detection works best when the full head and both eyes are clearly visible in a front-facing photo.
  • Very large, damaged or browser-incompatible files may not open or convert.
  • Automated checks cannot validate every lighting, background, expression, recency, printing or authority-specific requirement.

Frequently asked questions

Does PhotoSahi upload my photo?

No. The selected photo, HEIC conversion, face detection, crop, adjustment and JPEG encoding stay in your browser.

Can I use an iPhone HEIC photo?

Yes. PhotoSahi can convert HEIC and HEIF photos to a working image locally in the browser before processing.

Which applications have presets?

PhotoSahi includes presets for Canadian citizenship and passport photos, Indian passport reissue and surrender, OCI, PCC, LinkedIn and Microsoft Teams.

Does PhotoSahi guarantee that my photo will be accepted?

No. PhotoSahi provides preparation and framing assistance. The organization receiving the photo makes the final acceptance decision.

Is PhotoSahi free and does it require an account?

PhotoSahi is free to use and does not require signup.
